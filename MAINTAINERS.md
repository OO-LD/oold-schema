All three are at Fraunhofer ISC. The project is funded by the Prototype Fund of the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space.

A maintainer can merge, tag a release, and decide a normative question under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Adding one is a decision of the current maintainers, recorded in this file.

This list is deliberately short and is expected to grow. The project has no independent contributors with merge rights yet, and saying so is more useful than implying a wider bench.
