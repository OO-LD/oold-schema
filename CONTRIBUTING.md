# Contributing

The contributor guide is part of the documentation, so it is versioned and published with
everything else it describes:

- <https://oo-ld.org/latest/contributing/>
- source: [`docs/contributing.md`](docs/contributing.md)

In short: `spec/sections/` is the normative source, `make spec` rebuilds everything derived
from it, and `make check` is what CI runs.
